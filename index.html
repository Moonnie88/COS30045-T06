TV Energy Consumption

Energy Consumption for different TV screen types and sizes

TV's vary considerably in energy consumption.

The histogram only includes TVs with energy consumption less than 1,800 kWh per year.

TVs with same star rating can have very different energy consumption. It all depends on screen size and technology type. Hover over a point to see its screen size.
